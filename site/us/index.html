Good to know

Questions, answered

Is my financial data really private?

Yes. Your statements and holdings are stored only on your computer, in a single file you control. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and there's no account or login.

How much does it cost?

The app is free to download and use. The optional AI analysis runs on Claude; you can use a shared access code or your own key in Settings.

What can I import?

Account statements (PDFs, CSVs, Excel) from common brokers and platforms. US brokerage exports are supported natively.

Does it work for both India and the US?

Yes. Pick your region and Sampatti adapts the currency, instruments and tax rules — rupees and Indian assets, or dollars and US accounts.
